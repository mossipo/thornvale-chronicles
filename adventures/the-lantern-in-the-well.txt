== SCENE: well_yard
title: The Old Well Yard
area: wilds
rest: short
The farm at Copperfield Bend has stood empty for a year. Its well has not. Every night since the harvest, a light has burned at the bottom of it, and the farmer's widow, Ada Copperfield, wants to know why.

A rope is still tied to the well's iron crank. Below, the shaft drops into darkness, and very far down something glows like a coal.
back: The well yard is quiet. The rope still hangs down into the dark.

* Ask Ada what she has seen
  once
  text: "Lights, and humming," Ada says. "Not words. Just humming, the way my husband hummed when he worked. He fell down that well, you know. Before the harvest."
  set: heard_ada
* Test the rope before you trust it
  once
  check: athletics 10
  pass: The rope holds. It is newer than the crank it is tied to. Someone has been going up and down. ; xp: 10
  fail: The rope holds, but you skin your palms finding that out. ; damage: 1 slashing
* Climb down the rope
  go: well_shaft

== SCENE: well_shaft
title: Down the Shaft
area: well
rest: short
The shaft is slick with moss. Halfway down, the stones open into a dry side tunnel hung with webs as thick as wool. Below you, the glow grows brighter.
back: The mossy shaft, the webbed side tunnel, and the glow below.

* Cut through the webs into the side tunnel
  if: !webs_cleared
  fight: well_spider x2
  win: well_shaft ; set: webs_cleared
  flee: well_yard
* Search the side tunnel
  if: webs_cleared
  once
  check: investigation 11
  pass: Wrapped in old silk you find a farmer's purse and a stoppered vial. ; gold: 2d6 ; item: potion
  fail: Bones of rats and birds. Nothing else.
* Climb down toward the light
  check: athletics 11
  pass: drowned_room
  fail: The rope burns through your hands and you drop the last ten feet into cold water. ; damage: 1d6 bludgeoning ; go: drowned_room
* Climb back up to the yard
  go: well_yard

== SCENE: drowned_room
title: The Drowned Room
area: none
The bottom of the well opens into a stone room half full of black water. On a ledge sits a brass lantern burning with a steady golden light, though it holds no oil. Beside it, waist-deep in the water, stands a figure in a farmer's coat. Its face is grey, its eyes are milk-white, and it is humming.
back: The drowned watcher stands beside the lantern, humming.

* Hum his tune back to him
  if: heard_ada
  check: persuasion 13
  pass: You hum the tune back. The figure goes still. For a moment something like a man looks out of its white eyes. It lifts the lantern and holds it out to you, then sinks slowly into the water and is gone. ; xp: 100 ; item: brass_lantern ; go: well_end
  fail: The humming turns into a low, wet growl. ; fight: drowned_watcher ; noflee ; win: well_end
* Strike before it turns around
  check: stealth vs drowned_watcher
  pass: It never hears you over the water. ; fight: drowned_watcher ; surprise: player ; win: well_end
  fail: It turns. ; fight: drowned_watcher ; win: well_end
* Climb back up the shaft
  go: well_shaft

== SCENE: well_end
title: Copperfield Bend at Dawn
area: none
rest: both
You climb out into the grey dawn. Ada Copperfield is waiting at the well with a shawl around her shoulders. When she sees the brass lantern, she sits down on the edge of the well and cries, and then she laughs, and then she asks you in for breakfast.

* Take the lantern and give it to Ada
  if: !has brass_lantern, !lantern_given
  text: You lift the lantern from where it fell beside the watcher. It is still warm.
  item: brass_lantern
* Give the lantern to Ada
  if: has brass_lantern
  text: She holds it a long time. "He carried this into the mine every day for thirty years," she says. "Thank you for bringing him home." She presses five gold coins on you and will not take them back.
  take: brass_lantern
  gold: 5
  xp: 50
  set: lantern_given
  end
