THE ADVENTURE FILE FORMAT
A plain .txt file. Any text file without "== SCENE" lines opens in reader mode instead.

AT THE TOP
  TITLE: The Name of Your Adventure
  START: first_scene_id
  AUTHOR: optional

BLOCKS
Each block starts with a line like  == SCENE: id  (also MONSTER, ITEM, TABLE).
Ids are one word: letters, numbers, _ or -.

== SCENE: well_yard
  title: The Old Well Yard
  area: wilds       wilds, marsh, dungeon, crypt, tower, town, none, or a TABLE id
  rest: short       short, both (short and long), or none
  back: Text shown when you come back here later.
  Any other lines are the scene's text. A blank line starts a new paragraph.

  * Label of a choice
    if: flag, !flag, has item_id, gold>=10, level>=2   (all must be true)
    once                      the choice disappears after you pick it
    check: stealth 12         also: save dex 12 / str 12 / athletics or acrobatics 12
                              / stealth vs goblin / athletics contest 5
    pass: what happens on a success
    fail: what happens on a failure
    ...or effects directly on the choice:
    text: Narration.
    go: other_scene
    gold: 5   or   gold: 2d6        xp: 50       heal: 1d8
    item: potion   or   item: brass_lantern x2        take: brass_lantern
    pay: 10                         insp            (gain Inspiration)
    set: flag_name, other_flag      unset: flag_name
    damage: 1d6 piercing            damage: 2d6 fire save dex 12 half
    fight: goblin x2, wolf          surprise: player  or  surprise: enemy
    ambush: 4  (hidden foes roll Stealth +4 against your passive Perception)
    win: scene_id ; set: flag       flee: scene_id      noflee      rout: 1
    shop: odda   (marta, odda, pell)
    end          (marks the adventure complete)

  pass: and fail: lines hold several effects separated by " ; "
    pass: You find a purse. ; gold: 2d6 ; item: potion
    fail: It hears you. ; fight: drowned_watcher ; win: well_end
  A single word that matches a scene id means "go there":
    pass: drowned_room

== MONSTER: well_spider
  Either paste a stat block after a line that says  statblock:
  or write it out:
  name: Well Spider
  type: beast
  ac: 13
  hp: 11
  xp: 50
  pp: 10             passive Perception
  init: 3            Dexterity modifier
  attack: Bite +5 1d6+3 piercing      (one line per attack)
  multi: 2           attacks per turn
  saves: str+1 dex+3 con+1 int-4 wis+0 cha-3
  special: pack, nimble, martial
  vuln: radiant       immune: poison
  Fights can also use core monsters (goblin, wolf, skeleton, orc...) and
  anything in your Bestiary, by id or by name.

== ITEM: brass_lantern
  name: The Brass Lantern
  desc: What it looks like.
  heal: 2d4+2      (makes it a usable healing item)
  insp: yes        (using it grants Inspiration)
  ac: 1            (+1 AC while carried)
  quest: yes
  Core items work too: potion, poultice, ration, rope, torch, shield,
  w_longsword (weapons start with w_), a_chainmail (armor starts with a_).

== TABLE: well
  One row per line:   range | label | text | effects
  1-5 | Hostile | A spider drops on a silk line. | fight: well_spider
  9-15 | Quiet | Water drips.
  16-19 | Find | A coin in the moss. | gold: 1d6
  Use it with  area: well  in a scene, then "Explore the area" rolls on it.
